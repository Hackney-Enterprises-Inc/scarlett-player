Fix three inaccuracies in `viewEnd` seen on production pre-rolls. Watch and play time accrued only at heartbeats, so the time since the last one was dropped: with a 30 s heartbeat a 16 s pre-roll reported `playTime` 0, and a heartbeat credited its whole interval as play time if playback happened to be running at the tick. Time now accrues up to every playback, pause, stall and end transition and before both `viewEnd` payloads, so `viewEnd` carries the final partial interval and `playTime` excludes stalls and pauses to the millisecond. A completed view now reports `completionRate` 100 even when a host's own `ended` listener has already called `load()`, which zeroes `currentTime` and `duration`; any other view falls back to the last position reported by `playback:timeupdate` (or a heartbeat) while the duration was known, so a view switched away before its first heartbeat still reports how far it got. And the pause the media element fires just before `ended` is no longer reported as a viewer pause: no `pause` beacon, no `pauseCount` increment and no pause duration when `ended` follows it. A pause in the last half-second of VOD is held for one task to see whether `ended` follows; if not, it is counted as the viewer's with its original time and position.
